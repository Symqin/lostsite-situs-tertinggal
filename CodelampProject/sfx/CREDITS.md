# Sound effect credits

All gameplay sound effects below are free assets recorded/made by people and
published under **CC0 (public domain)** on OpenGameArt.org and Freesound.org. CC0 does not
require attribution, but we list the authors to thank them and so anyone can
re-check the sources. Files were only renamed, or trimmed to the audible part,
converted to 16-bit WAV and normalised; no AI-generated audio is used.
